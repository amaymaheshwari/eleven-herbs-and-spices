# ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
# SECTION 10: PRICING POWER AND ELASTICITY TESTING
# ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

**Fact.** WCN employs CPI-linked escalators in the majority of its municipal franchise contracts. Commercial contracts typically include annual price adjustment clauses tied to CPI, fuel surcharges, or fixed percentage increases (typically 3–5%).

### Pricing Governance

| Contract Type | Price Mechanism | Frequency | WCN Control Level |
|--------------|----------------|-----------|------------------|
| Municipal franchise | CPI-indexed or fixed-step | Annual | Contractually fixed |
| Commercial | Annual adjustment; negotiated | Annual | High (WCN proposes) |
| Disposal/Tipping fees | Market-based + premium for exclusive access | Variable | High in captive markets |
| ES | Project bid pricing + index adjustments | Per contract | Moderate |

**Analysis.** Pricing elasticity in solid waste is very low (inelastic demand). Waste collection is a legal requirement and public-health necessity—there is no substitution. Field observations and academic research (Tawil 1995; Repetto et al.) confirm price elasticity of demand for MSW collection services is approximately −0.1 to −0.3 (highly inelastic).

**Fact.** WCN achieved ~6.5% price/mix organic growth in 2023 and approximately 5.5–6.0% in 2024 (per quarterly earnings supplements), materially above CPI of ~3.4% in 2023 and ~2.6% in early 2025. This demonstrates genuine pricing power above inflation.

### ARPU Ceiling Estimate

- Residential ARPU: ~$25–30/month per household; ceiling estimated ~$40–45 before meaningful churn to self-haul or competitor
- Commercial ARPU: Highly variable by container size; no evidence of ceiling breach in WCN's markets

**Inference.** The primary pricing risk is political/regulatory: municipalities occasionally push back on above-CPI increases during contract renegotiations. However, given exclusive franchise structures, WCN's negotiating position remains strong unless a municipality chooses to municipalize waste services (rare: <1% occurrence historically).
