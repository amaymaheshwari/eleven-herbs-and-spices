**Fact.** Broadridge's customer base spans three primary segments:

| Segment | Est. # Customers | Revenue Contribution | Mission-Criticality |
|---|---|---|---|
| Broker-Dealers & Banks (ICS) | ~1,000+ | ~35% of total | Extreme (regulatory compliance) |
| Asset Managers & Mutual Funds | ~6,000+ funds | ~25% of total | High (investor reporting) |

| Wealth Management (Advisors/RIAs) | ~1,500+ firms | ~10% of total | Medium-High |

**Analysis.** Customer pain points and jobs-to-be-done:

**Broker-Dealers (ICS):** Core job = regulatory compliance for proxy voting, annual reports, prospectus delivery. Pain point = cost of printing/mailing, complexity of omni-channel delivery, ISE (Investor Self-Service) adoption. Broadridge's digital delivery platform reduces per-investor communication cost by ~60–80% vs. paper (Broadridge Digital Adoption Report, 2023). Switching cost: Any replacement requires re-wiring DTCC connectivity, rebuilding client record databases, retraining operations staff — estimated 18–36 months and $10–50M+ depending on firm size.

**Asset Managers (ICS):** Job = accurate, timely, regulatory-compliant fund communications. Pain point = SEC reporting modernization rules (Forms N-PORT, N-CEN, new fund report format effective November 2024) require significant systems upgrades. Broadridge is the incumbent processor for ~6,000+ mutual fund and ETF share classes.

**Capital Markets / Banks (GTO):** Job = real-time post-trade processing, settlement, reconciliation, fails management. Pain point = T+1 settlement compressed the operational window by 50%, forcing automation. Broadridge's BTCS platform handles STP for equities, fixed income, and derivatives. Switching cost: 24–48 months to migrate a Tier 1 bank's post-trade stack; estimated $50–200M in migration costs.

**Wealth Management (GTO):** Job = unified advisor desktop, portfolio management, rebalancing, fee billing. Pain point = fragmented legacy systems. Broadridge's Wealth Management Platform (UNX/BWAY) is competing against SS&C Advent, Envestnet, and Orion.

**Fact.** Broadridge reported its top-10 customers accounted for approximately 25% of total revenue in FY2024 (10-K). No single customer exceeded 10%.

**Inference.** The "do-nothing" prevalence (incumbent inertia) is exceptionally high in ICS — broker-dealers have used Broadridge for decades and the cost of change exceeds any plausible savings from alternatives. In GTO wealth, "do-nothing" is lower as firms actively seek modernization. The procurement blocker in GTO is Tier 1 bank internal program prioritization and regulatory approval of third-party vendors (typically 12–18 months for large banks).
