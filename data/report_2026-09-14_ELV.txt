**[Analysis]** Key switching-cost dynamics by segment:

- **Commercial ESI**: Switching costs are moderate at the employer level (annual RFP cycles, broker relationships, benefit admin integration). Individual members rarely choose their plan; employers do. **Procurement blocker**: Plan administrator inertia; proof required = cost savings with maintained quality metrics (HEDIS scores, member satisfaction).
- **Medicare Advantage**: Individual member choice annually during Annual Enrollment Period (AEP: Oct 15–Dec 7). Switching costs are lower (CMS mandates annual election); loyalty driven by supplemental benefits (dental, OTC, transportation) and Star Ratings. **Proof required**: ≥4.0 Star Rating to receive quality bonus payments (+5% benchmark).
- **Medicaid**: State procurement (multi-year contracts, typically 3–5 years). **Highest switching friction** for ELV because rebidding requires regulatory approval and incumbency advantage is substantial.

**[Fact]** ELV lost approximately 1.0–1.5 million Medicaid members in 2023–2024 due to post-COVID redetermination disenrollments (the "unwinding" of continuous enrollment protections). This was an industry-wide phenomenon — approximately 23 million individuals nationally lost Medicaid coverage during the unwinding period per CMS data published in January 2025.

**[Inference]** The residual Medicaid population post-redetermination is likely **higher acuity** (sicker, higher-cost members retained; healthier members lost), which partially explains the MLR deterioration observed in 2023–2025. This is a temporary adverse selection dynamic that should normalize as states complete rate resets.
