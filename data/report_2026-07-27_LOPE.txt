## Investment Thesis in One Paragraph

**[Analysis]** Grand Canyon Education is a pure-play Education Services Provider (ESP) whose entire revenue base consists of fees charged to Grand Canyon University (GCU), a private, non-profit university. LOPE operates under a long-term Master Services Agreement (MSA) with GCU that runs through 2035, generating highly predictable, enrollment-driven revenue with ~30% adjusted EBITDA (Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, and Amortization) margins, robust Free Cash Flow (FCF) conversion (>90%), and a capital-light, asset-light model. Regulatory risk — the U.S. Department of Education (ED) oversight of GCU's non-profit status — is the dominant overhang; our analysis suggests the market is overpricing this tail risk relative to probability-weighted outcomes.

## SECTION 10: PRICING POWER AND ELASTICITY TESTING

### Pricing Governance

**[Fact]** LOPE's revenue is a fixed percentage of GCU's tuition revenue under the MSA. GCU sets tuition; LOPE's revenue is mechanically derived. GCU has historically increased tuition 2–4% annually.

**[Analysis]** GCU's tuition pricing power is constrained by:
- Competition from other online universities (e.g., WGU (Western Governors University), ASU Online (Arizona State University Online), SNHU (Southern New Hampshire University))
- Federal financial aid limits (Pell Grant maximums, loan limits)
- Adult learner price sensitivity

**[Fact]** GCU's annual tuition for online programs is approximately $8,500–$9,000 per year (per GCU website, 2024–2025 academic year). This compares favorably to:
- ASU Online: ~$10,000–$12,000/year
- University of Phoenix: ~$10,000–$12,000/year
- WGU: ~$7,500/year (competency-based, lower comparison)

### Elasticity Evidence

**[Analysis]** GCU's below-market online tuition pricing suggests meaningful elasticity protection — price increases of 2–3% annually are unlikely to trigger demand destruction given the $500–$1,000/year absolute dollar impact on students. However, aggressive pricing (>5% per year) could trigger competitive defection to WGU.

### ARPU Ceiling Before Churn Inflects

**[Inference]** We estimate the ARPU (Average Revenue Per User/Student) ceiling for online programs is approximately $11,000–$12,000 per year (at or near ASU Online pricing). At current ~$8,500–$9,000, there is approximately 20–30% pricing headroom before demand meaningfully impairs. This supports 3–4 years of 3% annual increases without enrollment risk.
