### Mission-Criticality

**[Fact]** Core banking systems process deposit accounts, loans, transactions, and regulatory reporting in real time. Downtime has direct regulatory and reputational consequences. This makes core platforms among the most mission-critical enterprise software deployments in any industry. [Source: Celent "Core Banking Modernization," 2024; FFIEC IT Examination Handbook]

### Switching Costs (Quantified)

**[Analysis]** A core banking conversion for a $500M-asset community bank typically involves:
- **Direct cost**: $300K–$800K in vendor fees
- **Implementation labor**: 18–36 months of internal and consultant time
- **Opportunity cost / risk**: Data migration errors, temporary regulatory reporting disruptions, staff retraining
- **Total estimated cost**: $1M–$3M for mid-size community banks

This creates a structural "do-nothing" prevalence estimated at >85% of institutions in any 12-month period—meaning JKHY's renewal rate is intrinsically high regardless of satisfaction levels.

### Procurement Blocker

**[Inference]** The primary blocker is **board-level risk aversion to core system change**. Community bank boards (often non-technical) view core migrations as existential risks. The proof required to unlock a conversion is typically a successful reference client of similar size in the same state, with documented zero-downtime migration. This explains why JKHY's private-cloud migration program's reference client base is a critical sales tool.
