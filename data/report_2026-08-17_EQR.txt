1. **Life-stage transitions:** Marriage, family formation driving suburban/homeownership move (primary driver: ~35–40% of move-outs)

### Expansion Vectors

In multifamily, "expansion" means rent increases on renewal + ancillary revenue growth:
1. **Renewal rent increases:** Primary expansion lever; 3–5% annually in normalized markets
2. **Ancillary revenue:** Parking, storage, pet fees, package lockers — growing 5–10%/year
3. **New unit additions:** Acquisitions and development pipeline
4. **Revenue management optimization:** AI-driven pricing capturing additional 50–100 bps [Inference]

### Leading Churn Indicator (60–90 Days Ahead)

**[Analysis]** For EQR, the leading churn indicator is **notice-to-vacate rate** (NTV) — leases where residents provide 30–60 day move-out notice. A rising NTV rate 60–90 days before quarter-end predicts occupancy weakness and concession pressure. EQR monitors this internally but does not publicly disclose NTV rates.
