## Investment Thesis in One Paragraph

MercadoLibre is the dominant digital commerce and financial-services platform across Latin America, operating in markets collectively representing ~670 million people and exhibiting e-commerce penetration of just 8–12% versus 20–30% in developed markets. The company's integrated flywheel—marketplace, logistics (Mercado Envíos), fintech (Mercado Pago), credit (Mercado Crédito), advertising, and SaaS—creates compounding switching costs and data advantages that no regional competitor can replicate at scale. With the stock trading near a 25% discount to our mid fair value and multiple dated catalysts in the next 12–24 months, all four decision gates pass.
