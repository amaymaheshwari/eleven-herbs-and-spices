**WACC Calculation (Illustrative):**
- Cost of Equity: Risk-free rate 4.5% + Beta (1.3) × Equity Risk Premium (5.5%) = 4.5% + 7.2% = **11.7%**
- Cost of Debt (post-tax): ~5.5% × (1 - 21%) = **4.3%**
- Capital structure: ~40% equity, ~60% debt (economic)
- WACC: 0.4 × 11.7% + 0.6 × 4.3% = 4.68% + 2.58% = **~7.3%**

> **[Inference]** A WACC of 7.3% is appropriate in current conditions but is sensitive to: (1) rate environment — a 50 bps increase in risk-free rate adds ~0.65% to WACC, compressing DCF fair value ~8–10%; (2) beta re-rating upward if recession probability increases.
