## SECTION 10: PRICING POWER AND ELASTICITY TESTING

**Fact.** LCII demonstrated meaningful pricing power in 2021–2022, raising prices in excess of input cost inflation and expanding gross margins to 21.2% in 2021. In 2022–2023, as volumes collapsed and OEMs pushed back, price/mix became a headwind. [Source: LCII 2022–2023 10-Ks, MD&A sections.]

**Analysis.** LCII's pricing dynamics:
- **OEM pricing:** Negotiated annually or per-model cycle. LCII can raise prices by passing through documented input cost changes (steel, aluminum, foam, wood). True "discretionary" price increases are limited because OEMs have procurement power and dual-sourcing optionality for commodity components.
- **Aftermarket pricing:** Stronger pricing power; brand recognition and convenience (Lippert Dealer Direct) support 3–5% annual price increases on replacement parts.
- **Technology products (Furrion):** Weaker than expected; consumer electronics market is deflationary over time.

**Pricing governance:** LCII has a dedicated pricing committee; raw material pass-through mechanisms are contractually embedded for most OEM relationships. [Source: LCII Q2 2023 Earnings Call commentary.]

**ARPU ceiling before churn inflects:** For OEMs, if LCII CPU were to rise materially above ~$5,500–6,000 per towable unit (from ~$4,700–4,900 currently), OEMs would likely accelerate vertical integration efforts or dual-sourcing. This represents approximately 15–25% upside from current CPU levels before material substitution risk.

**Fact.** LCII paid total dividends of approximately $148M in 2023 and has maintained or increased the dividend through the downcycle, signaling management confidence in FCF durability. [Source: LCII 2023 Annual Report, cash flow statement.]

### Rule of 40

**Analysis.** LCII is an industrial manufacturer, not a SaaS company, so the Rule of 40 (revenue growth rate + FCF margin) is an imperfect but instructive metric:
- FY2023: Revenue growth –27% + FCF margin ~5.5% = Rule of 40 score = **–21.5%** (cyclical trough distortion).
- FY2024E recovery: Revenue growth ~0–5% + FCF margin ~6–7% = **+6–12%** (still trough).
- FY2025E recovery: Revenue growth ~8–15% + FCF margin ~7–9% = **+15–24%** (improving but industrial-grade, not software-grade).

### Key Accounting Judgments

1. **Goodwill impairment:** LCII carries approximately $800–900M in goodwill from acquisitions. 2. **Inventory valuation:** Rapid input cost changes (steel, aluminum) create LIFO/FIFO timing differences. LCII uses FIFO, which can overstate gross margin in a deflationary input environment and understate it in inflationary periods.
3. **Warranty accruals:** As a manufacturer, LCII accrues product warranty reserves. Changes in accrual methodology could swing EBIT by 50–100 bps.
