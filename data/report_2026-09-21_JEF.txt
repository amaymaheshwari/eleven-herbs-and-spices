## SECTION 1: THESIS FRAMING

**[Analysis]** Jefferies presents an asymmetric re-rating opportunity conditioned on: (1) the structural completion of a portfolio transformation from a diversified holding company into a pure-play investment bank; (2) a cyclical recovery in capital-markets activity after one of the deepest Investment Banking (IB) fee troughs since the Global Financial Crisis (GFC); and (3) sustained market-share gains in advisory and leveraged finance that are durable rather than episodic.

### Value-Creation Hurdle
**[Fact]** JEF must generate a Return on Tangible Equity (RoTE) of ≥ 12% sustainably (vs. ~8–10% trough in FY2023–FY2024) to justify a price-to-tangible-book-value (P/TBV) multiple of 1.3–1.5×, which would correspond to a fair value of ~$48–$56. At current levels (~$36), the market assigns roughly 1.0–1.05× TBV, implying "show-me" skepticism on earnings power. [Source: JEF FY2024 10-K; Bloomberg consensus, May 2025]

| 2. Capital-markets cycle recovery | IF M&A volumes and Equity Capital Markets (ECM) return to 2021 levels by H2 2026 | LSEG/Dealogic industry data; JEF backlog disclosures |

## SECTION 10: PRICING POWER AND ELASTICITY TESTING

**[Analysis]** Pricing power for investment banks is structurally limited by:
1. **Fee commoditization** at lower deal sizes (sub-$500M M&A)
2. **Competitive pressure** from boutiques and bulge brackets
3. **Client sophistication** — PE sponsors negotiate fee structures aggressively

### Advisory Fee Structure

**[Fact]** Typical M&A advisory fees follow the Lehman Formula (modified): approximately 1.0–1.5% of deal value for sub-$1B transactions; sliding scale to 0.25–0.5% for $5B+ deals. JEF's blended average fee per deal is estimated at ~0.5–0.8% based on disclosed deal counts and revenue. [Industry data: Freeman Consulting, Mergermarket 2024]

### Pricing Governance

**[Inference]** JEF has minimal disclosed pricing governance mechanisms. Fees are set deal-by-deal by senior bankers, creating potential for underpricing to win mandates. This is a structural weakness vs. firms with formalized pricing committees.

### Willingness-to-Pay

**[Analysis]** Willingness-to-pay is high for **differentiated advisory** (strategic, cross-border, complicated deals) and low for **commodity underwriting** (investment-grade DCM, plain-vanilla follow-on equity offerings). JEF must concentrate on the high-WTP (Willingness-to-Pay) segment.

### ARPU (Average Revenue Per User) Ceiling

**[Inference]** Per-deal revenue ceiling before client price resistance:
- Sub-$1B M&A: ~$8–12M per mandate
- $1–5B M&A: ~$15–35M per mandate
- $5B+ M&A: ~$25–60M per mandate (scale dependent)

JEF's current mix is heavily weighted toward the $500M–$3B range.
