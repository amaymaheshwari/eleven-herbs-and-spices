**[Analysis]** Build-Buy-Partner assessment:
- **Build**: Organic R&D spending ~2–3% of revenues — adequate for sustaining innovation, not transformative

**[Inference]** Capability gaps not buildable organically: (1) Steep-slope residential roofing (CSL is commercial-only) — could accelerate via acquisition; (2) Digital construction workflow integration — partnership more likely than acquisition.

**M&A pipeline**: CSL has not announced specific targets. Management commentary (Q4 2024 earnings call) indicated focus on bolt-on acquisitions in CCM adjacencies, targeting $200–500M deal size.
