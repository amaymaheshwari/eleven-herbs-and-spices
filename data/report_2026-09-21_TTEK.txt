**Fact.** The Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act (IIJA), enacted November 15, 2021, allocates $550B in new federal spending over five years (FY2022–FY2026). Water-specific allocations include:
- $55B for water infrastructure (clean water, drinking water State Revolving Funds (SRFs), and lead pipe replacement)

**Analysis.** TTEK is disproportionately positioned for water engineering (its core competency), program management, and environmental remediation — the three categories capturing the largest IIJA engineering professional services fees.
