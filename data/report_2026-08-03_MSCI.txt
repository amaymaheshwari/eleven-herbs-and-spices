### Workflow and Pain Points

**[Analysis]**

- **Asset owners**: Pain point = ESG/climate portfolio exposure quantification for reporting to beneficiaries and regulators.

### Switching Costs

**[Analysis]**

Switching costs are among the highest in financial data:

1. **Index segment**: Switching benchmark index requires fund prospectus amendments, shareholder notifications, and potential tracking error during transition. Estimated cost = $2–10M+ per large ETF; 12–24 month process.

3. **ESG data**: Clients build multi-year time-series of MSCI ESG ratings; switching disrupts comparability and historical analysis.
