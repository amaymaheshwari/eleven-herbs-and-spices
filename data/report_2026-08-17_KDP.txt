**[Fact]** KDP's pricing governance: A centralized revenue growth management (RGM) team sets pricing by SKU, channel, and geography. Trade promotion is managed via retailer-specific promotional calendars. KDP disclosed in Q1 2025 earnings that it has increased promotional support modestly in coffee to defend pod volume share.

**[Inference]** ARPU ceiling before churn inflects: Based on elasticity data, branded pod price above approximately $1.00/pod (vs. current $0.75–0.90) would likely trigger meaningful consumer switching to private label or alternative brewing. KDP appears aware of this, as 2025 price/mix guidance for coffee (+1%) is well below 2022–2023 levels.
